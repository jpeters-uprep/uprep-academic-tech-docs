title: Parental Control Apps?
---

## Question

Can I install a parental control or content-blocker app (like Qustodio or Net
Nanny) on my child's Chromebook? Does it cause issues logging in at school?

## Answer

Yes, if you are the owner of the Chromebook, you're welcome to install any
safety or parental control application you feel is necessary. UPrep does not
endorse or recommend a specific product.

If you're not already the device owner, see
[Chromebook Access Control](../../chromebook-access-control#device-controls) for
directions on making yourself the owner.

Keep in mind that our tech team cannot support or troubleshoot issues these apps
cause with logging in or running school apps — any conflicts will need to be
resolved at home.
