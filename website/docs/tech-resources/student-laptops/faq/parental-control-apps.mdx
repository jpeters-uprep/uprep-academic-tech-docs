## Question

Can I install a parental control or content-blocker app (like Qustodio or Net
Nanny) on my child's Chromebook? Does it cause issues logging in at school?

## Answer

Yes. If you're the owner of the Chromebook, you're welcome to install any safety
or parental control app you feel is necessary — UPrep doesn't endorse a specific
product, and the choice is entirely up to your family.

If you're not sure whether you're the device owner, or need to become the owner,
see
[Chromebook Access Control](/tech-resources/chromebook-access-control#2-device-owner-controls)
for how ownership works and how to set it.

A few things worth knowing before you install one:

- **Our tech team can't troubleshoot conflicts these apps cause at school.** If
  a parental control app blocks something your child needs for class or
  interferes with logging in, that's something to resolve at home, not something
  IT can fix on their end.
- **What gets affected depends on which account is active.** If your child signs
  in with their personal account (with their UPrep account added as secondary),
  a parental control app tied to the personal account can affect the whole
  device session, including while they're using their UPrep account for
  schoolwork. If they sign in directly with their UPrep account instead, most
  third-party parental control apps have much less reach, since UPrep's own
  Workspace policies govern that session.
- **Google Family Link works similarly, with real limits.** If you use Family
  Link instead of (or alongside) a third-party app, it's worth knowing exactly
  what does and doesn't carry over to your child's UPrep account — see
  [Family Link + School Account?](/tech-resources/student-laptops/faq/family-link-school-account)
  for the details.
