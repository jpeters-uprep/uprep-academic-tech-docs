## Question

I've added my child's UPrep account as a secondary account under Google Family
Link. Does that give me control over their school account too? Will screen time
or app blocks I set apply to their UPrep login?

## Answer

No. Adding the UPrep account as a secondary account to a Family Link-managed
personal account gives you a narrow set of conveniences, not general supervision
over the UPrep account.

Per
[Google's documentation](https://support.google.com/chromebook/answer/9900467?hl=en)
and confirmed by our own testing, here's what actually carries over to the UPrep
account when it's linked this way:

- Signing in to websites using the UPrep account
- Switching to the UPrep account to check email
- Switching to the UPrep account for a small set of allowed apps and extensions,
  like Google Classroom, to do schoolwork under parent supervision

That's the complete list. A few things notably don't carry over:

- **Screen time and downtime.** A screen time limit set in Family Link locks
  your child's personal account session on the device — it does not lock or
  affect their UPrep account. We tested this directly: after setting a screen
  time limit, the personal account locked as expected, while the UPrep account
  on the same Chromebook stayed fully usable.
- **App installs and Google Play.** While your child is signed in to their UPrep
  account, what they can install is controlled entirely by UPrep's Workspace
  admin console — Family Link has no visibility or veto over that, and there's
  no parent-approval prompt. This is also why UPrep can only force-install
  required class apps when a student is signed in directly with their UPrep
  account, not when it's added as secondary to a personal account.
- **Content filtering.** Sites blocked or allowed through Family Link apply to
  the personal account's browsing only.

In short: your personal Google account is fully parent-controlled, the UPrep
account is fully school-controlled, and linking them only grants the handful of
conveniences listed above — not a general parental control layer over the UPrep
account in either direction.
