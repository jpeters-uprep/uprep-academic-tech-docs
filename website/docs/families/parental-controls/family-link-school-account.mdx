## Question

I've added my child's UPrep account as a secondary account under Google Family
Link. Does that give me control over their school account too? Will screen time
or app blocks I set apply to their UPrep login?

## Answer

No. Adding the UPrep account as a secondary account to a Family Link-managed
personal account gives you a narrow set of conveniences — signing in to
websites, checking email, and using a few designated apps like Google Classroom
under parent supervision — not general supervision over the UPrep account.
Screen time limits, app installs, and content filtering all apply only to the
personal account, not the UPrep one.
