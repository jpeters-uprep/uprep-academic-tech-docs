## Question

I've added my child's UPrep account as a secondary account under Google Family
Link. Does that give me control over their school account too? Will screen time
or app blocks I set apply to their UPrep login?

## Answer

No. Adding the UPrep account as a secondary account inside your child's Family
Link-managed personal profile gives you a narrow set of conveniences — signing
in to websites, checking email, and using a few designated apps like Google
Classroom under parent supervision — not general supervision over the UPrep
account. Screen time limits, app installs, and content filtering all apply only
to the personal account, not the UPrep one.

This is separate from whether your child also has the **actual UPrep account**
set up as its own account on the device (added at the sign-in screen, not as a
secondary) — that's the normal, expected setup, and it's what lets required
class apps install automatically. That account is intentionally outside Family
Link's reach, governed by UPrep's own Workspace policy instead — see
[Recommended Setup](/families/chromebook-access-control#recommended-setup).
Adding UPrep as a secondary account is a convenience on top of that, not a
replacement for it.

See
[Linking the Two: What Actually Carries Over](/families/chromebook-access-control#linking-the-two-what-actually-carries-over)
for the full breakdown.
