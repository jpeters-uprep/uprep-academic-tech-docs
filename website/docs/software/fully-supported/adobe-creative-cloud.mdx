# Adobe Creative Cloud

UPrep faculty and all Upper School students have access to Adobe Creative Cloud,
a professional suite of design, media, and creative tools. Middle School
students have access if they are in certain classes such as photo, yearbook, or
journalism. Log in with your school email at https://creativecloud.adobe.com.

Students who are taking a student publications class also get access to Creative
Cloud through the school.

## Design & Illustration

Photoshop — Edit and enhance photos, create digital art, and composite images.

Illustrator — Design vector graphics, logos, and illustrations that scale
beautifully.

InDesign — Create posters, magazines, and multi-page documents for print or
digital use.

Express (formerly Spark) — Make quick graphics, flyers, and videos in your
browser.

Fresco — Digital drawing and painting app for tablets and stylus use.

## Photography

Lightroom — Edit and organize photos with easy, professional controls.

Lightroom Classic — Advanced photo cataloging and editing for desktop workflows.

Bridge — Browse, organize, and batch-rename Creative Cloud assets.

## Video, Audio & Motion

Premiere Pro — Full-featured video editing software for film and social media.

Premiere on iPhone — Simplified video editor for quick projects on mobile
(Adobe's successor to Premiere Rush, which was discontinued in 2026).

After Effects — Add motion graphics, titles, and visual effects.

Audition — Record, mix, and edit audio and voiceovers.

Media Encoder — Export and compress video and audio for sharing.

## Web, Animation & UX

Dreamweaver — Build and edit responsive websites with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## 3D & Immersive

Substance 3D Painter / Stager / Sampler / Designer / Modeler — Tools for
creating and rendering 3D textures, materials, and scenes. Free Substance 3D
education licenses require accredited higher education, not K-12 — check with
June before advertising this to students, since our entitlement may depend on a
different licensing tier.

## PDF, AI & Utility Tools

Acrobat Pro — Create, edit, and combine PDFs; add fillable forms and signatures.

Firefly (Generative AI) — Create AI-assisted images, text effects, and design
assets safely in the browser. Firefly ships on by default for K-12 (an admin can
disable it, all-or-nothing, per product) — see the
[AI Ethical Use Policy](/policies/technology-use-guidelines#ai-artificial-intelligence-ethical-use-policy)
for how AI-generated work should be handled at UPrep.

Character Animator — Bring illustrated characters to life with motion capture.

Capture (mobile) — Turn photos into color palettes, brushes, and vector shapes.

## Cloud & Collaboration

Creative Cloud Libraries — Save and share assets across apps.

Adobe Fonts — Thousands of fonts for Creative Cloud projects.

Adobe Stock — Royalty-free photos, graphics, and video clips for school use.

## Get Started

Visit https://creativecloud.adobe.com

Sign in with your @universityprep.org email and choose Company or School
Account.

Install the apps you need, or use Express, Firefly, and Photoshop Web directly
in your browser.
