For an overview of the built-in accessibility tools available on Apple,
ChromeOS, Android, and Microsoft devices, see
[Accessibility Tools](/students/accessibility-tools) — the device-level features
described there apply to your students just as much as to you.

## Faculty Guidance

Faculty are encouraged to:

- Provide digital copies of materials for use with assistive tools.

- Add alt text to images in slides and documents.

- Use clear headings and structured text for screen readers.

- Enable captions on videos shared in class.
