## Question

A YouTube video I inserted into Google Slides used to play fine (often without
ads), but now Slides says the content isn't available — even though the same
video plays normally if I go straight to YouTube. What's going on, and is there
a workaround?

## Answer

This isn't a problem with your slides or your Chromebook — it's YouTube.

Over the past year, YouTube has been closing off the ad-free ways that
third-party tools used to embed its videos. That's also why EdPuzzle stopped
being usable for us: it relied on the same kind of embedding. Playing a video
directly on youtube.com still works because that's the full, ad-supported
experience; the stripped-down embedded version many tools used is what's being
blocked or limited.

**This is a YouTube policy change, not something UPrep or Google Workspace
controls, and any workaround is likely temporary** — YouTube appears to be
closing these gaps one at a time.

A couple of things worth trying if a specific video won't play:

- Remove the video from the slide and re-insert it. This occasionally clears up
  the error on its own.
- If it's a video you or a colleague uploaded, check that **Allow embedding** is
  turned on for that video in YouTube Studio.

If neither helps, the realistic options going forward are:

- Play the video directly from youtube.com and accept that ads may run.
- For content you have the rights to, download the video file and insert it into
  the slide directly, instead of linking to YouTube.

There isn't a reliable ad-free trick left for embedding YouTube in Slides or
similar tools. If this is a recurring need for your class, reach out to Academic
Technology — we can help think through licensed, ad-free video options.
