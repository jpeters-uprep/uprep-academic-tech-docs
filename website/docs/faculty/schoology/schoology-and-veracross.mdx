Faculty use two separate systems that both involve grades, and it's a recurring
point of confusion which one is "official." Short version:

- **Veracross holds the official grades.** Term/final grades and narrative
  comments are entered directly in Veracross — see
  [Teacher Grades and Comments](/faculty/veracross/teacher-grades-and-comments).
  This is what goes on the report card.
- **The Schoology gradebook is your day-to-day gradebook** — assignments,
  formative work, and ongoing feedback that students and parents can check
  throughout the term. See
  [Gradebook](/faculty/schoology/best-practices/gradebook).
- **These two systems are not automatically synced.** Entering a grade in the
  Schoology gradebook does not populate Veracross, and vice versa — they're
  separate tools you populate separately, for different purposes.
- **Veracross is the authoritative roster.** It does not automatically sync
  mid-year schedule changes into Schoology — see
  [What do I do if a student is added or dropped from my class?](/faculty/schoology/faq/adding-dropping-students)
  for what to do when a student adds or drops.

For who to ask about which system, see [Who Owns What](/about/who-owns-what).
