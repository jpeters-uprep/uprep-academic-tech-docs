For UPrep's minimum expectations for Schoology use, see
[Schoology Requirements](/faculty/schoology/requirements). This section covers
implementation tips and additional features to help you meet — and go beyond —
those requirements.

**If you're new to Schoology, read these three first:**
[Course Organization](./course-organization),
[Assignments and Materials](./assignments-and-materials), and
[the Gradebook](./gradebook).

## Topics

- **[Course Organization](./course-organization)** — folder structure, the
  Unpublish/Archive distinction, and the Updates thread.
- **[Assignments and Materials](./assignments-and-materials)** — setting up
  assignments, the Submissions toggle, and attaching resources.
- **[Due Dates and Calendaring](./due-dates-and-calendaring)** — what must be
  calendared and when to post homework.
- **[Workload Calendar](./workload-calendar)** — cross-posting to the
  grade-level calendar.
- **[Gradebook](./gradebook)** — why UPrep uses Schoology's gradebook, Grade
  Setup, and Gradebook Exceptions (flags).
- **[Rubrics](./rubrics)** — building and reusing rubrics.
- **[Course Headers](./course-headers)** — an optional banner-style header for
  your course page.
- **[Reusing Your Materials](./reusing-your-materials)** — the year-end to
  year-start cycle for carrying your course over.
