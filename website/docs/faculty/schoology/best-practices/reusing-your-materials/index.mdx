When a term or year ends, your course materials don't have to start from scratch
next time. The cycle works in three steps, in this order:

1. **[Working in Resources](./working-in-resources)** — Resources is a storage
   area (and work area) for your materials, separate from any one course.
2. **[Saving to Resources](./saving-to-resources)** — save your current course's
   materials into Resources at the end of a term or year.
3. **[Adding Resources to a Course](./adding-resources-to-a-course)** — when a
   new course opens, import your saved materials into it.

## Coming from Another LMS?

If you have course content from another system that exports Common Cartridge
files (like Canvas), you don't need to rebuild it by hand — Schoology's
[Common Cartridge Converter](https://uc.powerschool-docs.com/en/schoology/latest/common-cartridge-converter)
can import it directly into your Resources.
