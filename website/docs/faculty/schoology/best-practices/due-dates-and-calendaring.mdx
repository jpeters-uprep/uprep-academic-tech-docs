The class calendar is the most common time management and organizational tool
that students use to organize their tasks, so complete and accurate due dates
make a real difference for student organization. See
[Schoology Requirements](/faculty/schoology/requirements) for which assignments
must be calendared.

## When to Post Homework

Since students consult Schoology as a way to plan their homework for the
evening, consider when you post new homework into Schoology. Students tell us,
for example, that it's most useful when the homework is posted during class or
immediately after school, even if it's been discussed in class. Posting homework
late at night for the next day may miss students if they've already completed
their home study time for the day.
