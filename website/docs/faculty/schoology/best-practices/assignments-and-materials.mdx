There are many options in setting up an assignment within Schoology. While these
may seem daunting at first, they are each important to make sure that both
faculty and students can access the information we need reliably and
efficiently.

[Schoology's Assignments Quick Reference Card](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1AD5bXMG0COHQjTswWqLrsKcv8G2J2cNN/view?usp=sharing)

## Material Types

Students rely on Schoology as their primary resource for their upcoming due
dates and homework obligations. Please create an entry in Schoology for any
due-date student expectations, even if you plan on collecting the work
physically or offline. See
[Schoology's Course Materials documentation](https://uc.powerschool-docs.com/en/schoology/latest/course-materials-instructors)
for full details on each type; below are our UPrep-specific notes.

**Assignments** are the most flexible and commonly used type of Schoology
material at UPrep.

**[Discussions](https://uc.powerschool-docs.com/en/schoology/latest/course-materials-discussions-instructors)**
are commonly used for peer-review assignments, where you want students to be
able to see and comment upon each other's work.

**[Media Albums](https://uc.powerschool-docs.com/en/schoology/latest/course-materials-media-albums-instructors)**
let students submit photos, video, or audio, with optional comments visible to
the class. Useful for presentations, performances, or visual work that doesn't
fit a written submission.

**[Quiz/Test](https://uc.powerschool-docs.com/en/schoology/latest/course-materials-tests-quizzes-instructors)**
questions are often used as formative checks, since they can be assigned to a
specific date/time, or left open with a due date.

Schoology also supports **External Tool (LTI)**, **SCORM/web content**,
**Page**, **File/Link**, and **Folder** material types — Events (calendar-only
entries with no submission or grade) are a separate calendar object, not a
course material type. Adjacent features worth knowing about: **Conditional
Release**, **Individually Assign**, **Student Completion Rules**, and
**Portfolios**.

## Submissions

By default, Schoology assumes that assignments will be submitted electronically.
If you don't want students to submit this electronically (for example, there's a
handout or worksheet), turn off **Submissions** — listed under "Assignment
option details" alongside **Count in Grade**, **Individually Assign**, **Copy to
Courses**, and **PowerBuddy**. Otherwise, Schoology will assume that the
assignment is missing, and erroneously notify students that their work is
missing. This has the unfortunate side effect of training students to ignore all
missing assignment notifications, whether accurate or not.

## Attaching Resources

Schoology allows you to attach links or supplemental files to an assignment. If
there are files or websites which a student should use to complete an
assignment, please attach them directly to the assignment so that students may
easily find them. If the resource will be used beyond that assignment, for
example throughout a unit or throughout the term, consider putting it in the
most appropriate folder and leaving instructions on where students will find it.
