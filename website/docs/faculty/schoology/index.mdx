Schoology is UPrep's learning management system, used for coursework,
assignments, grading, and communication. Academic Technology (June) administers
Schoology; ILT sets the requirements every course must meet. (The vendor's
current product name is "Schoology Learning" — you may see that name in some
PowerSchool materials. No migration or end-of-life has been announced.)

## Three Doors

- **[Schoology Requirements](./requirements)** — What every course must do.
  Owned by ILT.
- **[Best Practices](./best-practices)** — How to do it well, and the features
  worth knowing.
- **[Schoology FAQ](./faq)** — Something's broken or missing.
