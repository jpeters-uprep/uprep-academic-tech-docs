These are UPrep's minimum expectations for using Schoology — the baseline every
course should meet, regardless of subject or division. This information is owned
by ILT. For tips on implementation, see
[Best Practices](/faculty/schoology/best-practices).

## Both Divisions

- Due dates for all items must be calendared on the course Schoology page. Due
  dates must align with class periods.
- All courses must have a syllabus on their Schoology page
- Any materials students need to complete work outside of class (readings,
  worksheets, rubrics, etc.) should be available to students electronically via
  Schoology whenever possible, and placed where they are easy to find.
- Respond to student updates and replies sent through Schoology within one
  working day, if follow up is needed.
- Links or files needed for assignments must be linked in the assignment
  directly.
- If your class has linked or cross-listed sections on a rotating schedule, make
  sure due dates are calendared accurately for each section — use Grading Groups
  as necessary in combined classes. See
  [How do I add a section to my class?](/faculty/schoology/faq/add-section) and Schoology's
  [Grading Groups documentation](https://uc.powerschool-docs.com/en/schoology/latest/grading-groups)
  for more.
- Schoology gradebook:
  - Make sure any information you share with students via the Schoology
    gradebook is accurate — review how your
    [Grade Setup](https://uc.powerschool-docs.com/en/schoology/latest/courses-grade-setup)
    is configured to confirm it's doing what you expect. Your Department Chair
    or June can help you check your setup.
- Turn Submissions ON when you want students to be able to submit work through
  Schoology, and OFF for everything else — leaving it on unintentionally
  triggers unnecessary missing-work alerts for students (see
  [Submissions](/faculty/schoology/best-practices/assignments-and-materials#submissions)).

## Upper School

- In gradebook setup, check the box "Round Period/Final Grades."

## Middle School

- All graded assignments, projects, and assessments must appear in Schoology.
  Students, advisors, and parents should be able to view grades for all assessed
  work. Teachers can maintain separate grade books; however, Schoology must
  still be used to record grades.
- In gradebook setup, under Visibility Settings, check the box "Hide Overall
  grade..." and "Hide grading period grade..."
