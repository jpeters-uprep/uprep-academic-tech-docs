## Question

I tried to fix the name on my course, but my edit either didn't stick or
changed the name for everyone else teaching the same course. What happened?

## Answer

Schoology courses have two separate name fields, and it's easy to edit the
wrong one:

- **Course Name** — the big title (e.g. "Topics in US History"). This is
  shared across **every section** of that course, including sections taught
  by other teachers. Editing it changes the name everywhere the course
  appears for everyone.
- **Section Name** — the smaller label under the course title (e.g.
  "HIST207-S1-IM-2 Meyer"). This is specific to **your section only** and is
  where your name, period, or other section-specific identifiers belong.

If you add your name or initials to the Course Name field instead of the
Section Name field, it will show up correctly for you — but it also renames
the course for every other teacher's section, and someone else's manual
correction (or the next sync) can just as easily overwrite your edit back to
whatever the shared Course Name was.

To fix a section-specific name:

1. Go to **My Courses** and open the course, then click **Edit Course**.
2. On the **Info** tab, leave **Course Name** alone — only touch it if you
   mean to rename the course for every section.
3. Edit the **Section Name** field for your own section instead.
4. Click **Save**.

If the Course Name itself is wrong (not just your section), or you're not
sure which one to change, email
[registrar@universityprep.org](mailto:registrar@universityprep.org) or
[June](mailto:jpeters@universityprep.org) rather than guessing — a Course
Name edit affects everyone teaching that course.
