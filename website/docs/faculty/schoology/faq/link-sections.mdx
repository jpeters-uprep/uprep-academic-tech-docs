## Question

I teach more than one section of the same course. Can I link them together so
I only have to build materials once?

## Answer

Yes — Schoology calls this **linking sections**. Once two or more sections of
the same course are linked, an assignment (or other material) you create in
one is automatically copied to all of the linked sections, so you build it
once instead of repeating the work per section.

What stays shared and what stays separate:

- **Materials are shared.** Create an assignment once and it's copied to every
  linked section. You can still choose which of the linked sections a given
  piece of material goes to instead of sending it to all of them.
- **Gradebooks and enrollment stay separate.** Each section keeps its own
  roster and its own gradebook — linking materials doesn't merge grades or
  students across sections.

To link sections, all of the following have to be true:

- The sections belong to the **same course** (same course code).
- The sections are in the **same grading period**.
- The sections have the **same Course Admin(s)**.
- **No student is enrolled in more than one** of the sections you're linking.

**Combining sections that have different course codes** (sometimes called
cross-listing — for example, two different courses that meet together) is a
system-admin-only action. [E-mail June](mailto:jpeters@universityprep.org) if
that's what you need.

### Do this before the term starts

Link sections **before** you've built out materials or entered grades, not
partway through the term. Linking sections after a course already has content
can lose existing course content, grades, or enrollment data. If you're mid-term
and think your sections should be linked, email
[June](mailto:jpeters@universityprep.org) rather than linking them yourself.

### How to link sections

1. Confirm you're a Course Admin on all the sections involved — see
   [How do I become a course admin?](../become-course-admin)
2. From **Members** or the course's section list, use the gear icon next to a
   section to find the linking option, per Schoology's
   [Section Linking documentation](https://uc.powerschool-docs.com/en/schoology/latest/linking-sections-with-cross-listing).
3. If the option isn't available to you, the system-level permission that
   allows Course Admins to link sections may not be turned on —
   [e-mail June](mailto:jpeters@universityprep.org) to have it enabled or to
   have the sections linked for you.
