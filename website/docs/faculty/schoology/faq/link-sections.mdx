## Question

I teach more than one section of the same course. Can I link them together so
I only have to build materials once?

## Answer

Yes — Schoology calls this **linking sections**. Once two or more sections of
the same course are linked, an assignment (or other material) you create in
one is automatically copied to all of the linked sections, so you build it
once instead of repeating the work per section.

What stays shared and what stays separate:

- **Materials are shared.** Create an assignment once and it's copied to every
  linked section. You can still choose which of the linked sections a given
  piece of material goes to instead of sending it to all of them.
- **Gradebooks and enrollment stay separate.** Each section keeps its own
  roster and its own gradebook — linking materials doesn't merge grades or
  students across sections.

To link sections, all of the following have to be true:

- The sections belong to the **same course** (same course code).
- The sections are in the **same grading period**.
- The sections have the **same Course Admin(s)**.
- **No student is enrolled in more than one** of the sections you're linking.

**Combining sections that have different course codes** (sometimes called
cross-listing — for example, two different courses that meet together) is a
system-admin-only action. [E-mail June](mailto:jpeters@universityprep.org) if
that's what you need.

### What happens if the sections already have content

Link sections **before** you've built out materials or entered grades if at
all possible — Schoology's own documentation warns that linking sections
after a course already has content "can result in the loss of course content,
grades, and enrollment data." Concretely, here's what that means:

- **Grades are protected.** Schoology forces a CSV export of each section's
  existing gradebook before the link completes, so a snapshot of grades up to
  that point is preserved on your computer even if something goes wrong.
- **Other student work is not guaranteed to survive.** Materials, submissions,
  and discussion posts that already exist in the sections being linked are not
  reliably carried over — other schools that have linked populated sections
  report that existing student work can be lost permanently in the process.
  Anything created **after** the link is in place behaves normally (shared
  and copied to every linked section, per above).
- **It's effectively one-way.** Unlinking sections afterward doesn't cleanly
  restore them to their pre-link state — treat linking as a decision you're
  not going to reverse.

So: if a section already has real content in it, don't link it purely to save
setup time — you're trading a known amount of duplicate work now for an
unclear amount of data loss. Two options that don't require emailing anyone:

- **Export your gradebook first.** From each section, use Gradebook → Export
  to save your own CSV backup before doing anything else, regardless of what
  Schoology's own forced export captures.
- **Recreate the essentials in one section, then drop the rest.** If the
  sections are lightly populated (a syllabus, a couple of assignments), it's
  usually faster and safer to manually rebuild that handful of items in the
  section you're keeping than to link and hope everything survives.

If a section has substantial content you can't afford to lose (a full term of
assignments, submissions, and grades), the safe answer is: don't link it.
Export your gradebook, keep the sections separate, and accept the duplicate
upkeep — that's less costly than risking a term of student work.

### How to link sections

1. Confirm you're a Course Admin on all the sections involved — see
   [How do I become a course admin?](../become-course-admin)
2. From **Members** or the course's section list, use the gear icon next to a
   section to find the linking option, per Schoology's
   [Section Linking documentation](https://uc.powerschool-docs.com/en/schoology/latest/linking-sections-with-cross-listing).
3. If the option isn't available to you, the system-level permission that
   allows Course Admins to link sections may not be turned on —
   [e-mail June](mailto:jpeters@universityprep.org) to have it enabled or to
   have the sections linked for you.
