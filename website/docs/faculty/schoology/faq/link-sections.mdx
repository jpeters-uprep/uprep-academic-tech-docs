## Question

I teach more than one section of the same course. Can I link them together so
I only have to build materials once?

## Answer

Yes — Schoology calls this **linking sections**, and it works around a
**master section / child section** structure: you pick one section to be the
master, and the others become children linked to it. From then on, an
assignment (or other material) you create in the master section is
automatically copied to all of its child sections, so you build it once
instead of repeating the work per section.

What stays shared and what stays separate:

- **Materials are shared, from the master outward.** Create an assignment in
  the master section and it's copied to every child section. You can still
  choose which child sections a given piece of material goes to instead of
  sending it to all of them.
- **Gradebooks and enrollment stay separate.** Each section keeps its own
  roster and its own gradebook — linking materials doesn't merge grades or
  students across sections.

To link sections, all of the following have to be true:

- The sections belong to the **same course** (same course code).
- The sections are in the **same grading period**.
- The sections have the **same Course Admin(s)**.
- **No student is enrolled in more than one** of the sections you're linking.

**Combining sections that have different course codes** (sometimes called
cross-listing — for example, two different courses that meet together) is a
system-admin-only action. [E-mail June](mailto:jpeters@universityprep.org) if
that's what you need.

### What happens if the sections already have content

Which section keeps its content is consistent — it's whichever one you
designate as the **master section**:

- **The master section keeps everything.** Its materials, setup, grades, and
  data all survive, and become the shared set that copies out to the child
  sections going forward.
- **Child sections lose their own pre-existing content.** Whatever materials,
  grades, or data a section already had before you made it a child are not
  recoverable — this is true whether you link or later unlink.
- **Student assignment submissions in a child section are also not
  recoverable.** This isn't limited to teacher-created materials.

In other words, linking isn't a merge — it's "pick a winner." So:

- **Pick the master deliberately.** Make it the section with the most content
  already built out, or the one you'd rather keep working in — not just
  whichever one happens to be listed first.
- **Export your gradebook from every section first**, from Gradebook →
  Export, before linking anything. This is your own backup, independent of
  whatever Schoology does internally.
- **If a child section has real content you can't afford to lose** (its own
  assignments, submissions, grades), the safe answer is: don't link it.
  Export its gradebook, keep it separate, and accept the duplicate upkeep —
  that's less costly than losing that section's work.
- Link **before** any of the sections have real content whenever you can —
  that sidesteps this decision entirely.

### How to link sections

1. Confirm you're a Course Admin on all the sections involved — see
   [How do I become a course admin?](../become-course-admin)
2. Decide which section will be the master section (see above).
3. From the course's section list, click the gear icon next to the section
   you want as master and choose the option to link child sections to it, per
   Schoology's
   [Section Linking documentation](https://uc.powerschool-docs.com/en/schoology/latest/linking-sections-with-cross-listing).
4. If the option isn't available to you, the system-level permission that
   allows Course Admins to link sections may not be turned on —
   [e-mail June](mailto:jpeters@universityprep.org) to have it enabled or to
   have the sections linked for you.
