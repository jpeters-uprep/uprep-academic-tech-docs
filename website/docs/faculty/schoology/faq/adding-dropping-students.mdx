## Question

A student's schedule changed after the start of the year — do I need to do
anything in Schoology?

## Answer

Yes. Veracross is always the official roster; Schoology does not automatically
sync mid-year schedule changes. If a student adds or drops your course, go to
the Members tab of your Schoology course and add or remove them yourself so your
Schoology roster matches Veracross.

Two things to know: students can't remove themselves from a course, and a
student can't be added to a new section of a class while still enrolled in an
old section of the same class — if you can't find a student in the add box,
check whether they're still enrolled in another section first.

Note: as of a June 2026 Schoology change, a student dropped via the SIS feed
goes **Inactive** rather than being removed outright, which preserves their
grades and past submissions. This is a platform-side change to how SIS drops
behave generally — it doesn't change what you need to do above for a manual
add/drop in your own course.
