Schoology now ships **PowerBuddy**, an AI assistant built into the platform — it
can appear as a named assignment option, and (where enabled) support student
chat and content generation from PDF/Word uploads.

**PowerBuddy is off entirely at UPrep.** It is not enabled for any role or grade
level. If you see a PowerBuddy option appear anywhere in Schoology, that's
unexpected — let June know.

This is separate from UPrep's general policy on AI tools, which still applies to
any other AI text- or image-generation tool students or faculty use for
coursework — see the
[AI Ethical Use Policy](/policies/technology-use-guidelines#ai-artificial-intelligence-ethical-use-policy)
in the Technology Use Guidelines.
