"Why is Schoology emailing my student so much?" and "why isn't it notifying them
at all?" are two of the most common Schoology questions faculty and families
ask. Most of the answer comes down to two mechanics:

## What Triggers an Overdue Notification

An assignment past its due date with no submission and no score generates an
Overdue notification.

## What Clears One

A submission, a manually entered grade, an **Excused** or **Missing** exception,
the course being archived, or **Enable Submission** being turned off for that
assignment. See
[Gradebook Exceptions (Flags)](/faculty/schoology/best-practices/gradebook/exceptions-and-flags)
for what each exception does.

## Why the Submissions Toggle Matters Most

If you leave **Submissions** on for an assignment you don't actually want
submitted electronically, Schoology treats it as missing the moment the due date
passes — even if you collected it in person. This is the single most common
cause of false "missing work" alerts, and it trains students to ignore the
notification entirely, accurate or not. See
[Assignments and Materials](/faculty/schoology/best-practices/assignments-and-materials)
for how to turn it off.

## Student and Family Notification Settings

Students and families can adjust how often (and whether) they're notified in
their own Schoology account settings. See
[Schoology for Students](/students/schoology-for-students) and
[Schoology Overview for Families](/families/schoology-for-families).
