Terms used elsewhere on this site that aren't spelled out where they appear.

### AUE (Auto Update Expiration)

The date Google stops shipping ChromeOS software updates for a specific
Chromebook model. Worth checking against your student's remaining years at UPrep
before buying a Chromebook — see
[Middle School Chromebooks](/families/middle-school-chromebooks).

### Course Admin

A Schoology role with administrative permissions over a specific course —
editing content, managing settings, and adding other admins. You need to be a
Course Admin on a course to copy resources into it. See
[How To Become Course Admin](/faculty/schoology/faq/become-course-admin).

### CT (Community Time)

UPrep's block of time for clubs, study hall, and student activities, including
Makerspace access. See
[Programs, Teams, and Clubs](/makers/programs-teams-and-clubs).

### Grading Groups

A Schoology feature that lets a single course have different due dates or
grading periods per class period or section, so a rotating or block schedule
still calendars correctly. See
[Schoology Requirements](/faculty/schoology/requirements).

### ILP (Individual Learning Plan)

A plan that lists the accommodations a student is entitled to receive and the
instructional strategies that best support them in the classroom. ILPs identify
learning strengths and challenges, specify accommodations, and outline
supportive instructional strategies. See UPrep's
[Integrated Learning](https://www.universityprep.org/academics/integrated-learning)
page for more.

### ILT (Instructional Leadership Team)

UPrep's leadership body for academic policy. ILT owns the Schoology
[Requirements](/faculty/schoology/requirements) and reviews annual software
purchase decisions — see [Who Owns What](/about/who-owns-what).

### ITIP (Individualized Teaching Improvement Program)

UPrep's structure for faculty to engage in self-selected professional
development.

### MS / US

Middle School / Upper School — used throughout this site to mark guidance that
differs by division.

### Paw Ratings

A workload-estimate system used to flag how much time an assignment is expected
to take, indicated with a number of \*\*\* in the assignment title. See
[Paw Ratings](/faculty/schoology/best-practices/paw-ratings).

### ULab

UPrep's Makerspace (L220), home to 3D printing, laser cutting, and other Makers
tools. See [Makers](/makers).

### Workload Calendar

A separate Schoology class for each grade level (9–12) that aggregates
assignments and assessments across all of that grade's classes, so faculty can
see the overall picture of what students are balancing at any given time. See
[Workload Calendar](/faculty/schoology/best-practices/workload-calendar).
