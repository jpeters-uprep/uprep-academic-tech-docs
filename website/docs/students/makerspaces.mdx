UPrep's Makerspaces — the Middle School and Upper School Makerspace, the
Library, and Stagecraft — are open to students for 3D printing, laser cutting,
robotics, and more.

See [Makers](/makers) for the full list of tools and equipment, and
[Programs, Teams, and Clubs](/makers/programs-teams-and-clubs) for robotics
teams, Makers Club, and Community Time access.
