Middle School students use a Chromebook, and Upper School students use a laptop,
for daily class work. Device requirements and purchasing decisions are made by
families — see the guides below for the specifics:

- **Middle School:**
  [Middle School Chromebooks](/families/middle-school-chromebooks)
  (requirements) and [Chromebook Setup](/families/chromebook-setup)
- **Upper School:** [Upper School Laptops](/families/upper-school-laptops)
  (requirements) and
  [Upper School Laptop Setup](/families/upper-school-laptop-setup)

## Something Not Working?

Check the [Families FAQ](/families/faq) for backups, Wi-Fi, passwords, and
printing, or contact [IT Support](https://support.universityprep.org/) for
device repair, loaners, and account issues.
