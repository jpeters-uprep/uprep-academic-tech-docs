[Schoology](https://universityprep.schoology.com/home) is where UPrep runs most
of its coursework — assignments, due dates, grades, and course materials all
live there.

## Finding Your Courses

Your courses show up in two places:

- **Courses menu** (top of the page) — a quick drop-down list. It only shows
  your first 12 courses; if a class is missing, see
  [What do I do if my course list is too long?](/students/schoology-for-students/course-list-too-long)
  for how to reorder or search.
- **Course Dashboard** (your Schoology home page) — shows all of your courses as
  tiles, which you can drag into any order you like.

If your Schoology homepage has a **Student Assignments** tab, that's another
place to see all of your assignments across courses in one list — not every
school has this feature turned on.

## Calendar and Upcoming

Your Schoology home page includes a Calendar and a To Do widget that pull
together due dates across every course, so you don't have to check each class
separately. See
[How do I know the dates for upcoming assignments and tests?](/students/schoology-for-students/upcoming-assignments-and-tests)
for both places to look.

## Submitting Work

If a teacher has turned on electronic submission for an assignment, you'll see a
place to upload or submit your work directly on the assignment page. If they
haven't, follow whatever in-person instructions your teacher gives — Schoology
may still show the assignment and due date even when nothing is collected
online.

## Feedback and Grades

Grades and comments from your teacher show up in the Gradebook, and sometimes
directly on your submission. See
[Where can I see my grades?](/students/schoology-for-students/where-can-i-see-my-grades) for
where to look, both per-course and across all courses.

If you see a flag (like **Missing**, **Incomplete**, or **Exempt**) next to an
assignment instead of a score, see
[Gradebook Exceptions (Flags)](/faculty/schoology/best-practices/gradebook/exceptions-and-flags)
for what each one means.

## Notifications

Schoology can email or push-notify you about new grades, due dates, and
messages. Check your notification settings in Schoology if you're getting too
many emails, or too few.

## Mobile App

Schoology has apps for iOS and Android, in addition to the website.
