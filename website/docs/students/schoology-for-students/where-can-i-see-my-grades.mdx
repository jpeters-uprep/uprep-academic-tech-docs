## Question

Where can I see my grades?

## Answer

Grades and comments from your teacher show up in the Gradebook, and sometimes
directly on your submission.

To see your grades for a specific course, open that course and click
**Grades** in the left-hand course menu. It breaks your grade down by
category (like Homework or Tests) and shows your overall course grade at the
bottom.

<img
  src={useBaseUrl("/img/schoology-walkthrough/SeeGrades.png")}
  width="100%"
  alt="Grades page for a course, showing grade categories and the overall course grade"
/>

You can also click **Grades** in the top menu bar to see grades across all of
your courses at once.

If you see a flag (like **Missing**, **Incomplete**, or **Exempt**) next to an
assignment instead of a score, see
[Gradebook Exceptions (Flags)](/faculty/schoology/best-practices/gradebook/exceptions-and-flags)
for what each one means.
