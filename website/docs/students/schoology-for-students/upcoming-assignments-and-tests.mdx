## Question

How do I know the dates for upcoming assignments and tests?

## Answer

There are two places on your Schoology home page that pull together due dates
across every course, so you don't have to check each class separately.

### 1. The To Do widget

On your Schoology home page, click **Course Dashboard** (next to Recent
Activity). The **To Do** box on the right lists your upcoming assignments and
tests, with due dates and which course each one belongs to.

/>

### 2. The Calendar

Click the calendar icon in the top menu bar to see every upcoming assignment
and test plotted on a monthly calendar, across all of your courses at once.

  src={useBaseUrl("/img/schoology-walkthrough/SeeUpcomingCalendar.png")}

  alt="Schoology Calendar showing upcoming assignments and tests by due date"
/>
